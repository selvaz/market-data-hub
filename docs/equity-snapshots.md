# Equity snapshots

`run_etf_valuations.py --db <absolute-path>` and `run_market_breadth.py --db <absolute-path>` collect free, daily snapshots. They use the shared database write lock and download log. Proposed Windows task times are 13:40 and 13:50 Pacific respectively, after the US close. The registration scripts create disabled tasks; deployment and enabling are separate decisions.

Yahoo quote `trailingPE`, `forwardPE`, `priceToBook` and `trailingAnnualDividendYield` are captured directly; yield is a fraction. Missing fields are NULL. The quote fields are not necessarily calculated as of the collection date. Yahoo does not provide a reliable valuation as-of date in this response, so `source_as_of` is NULL. The raw field names and the `topHoldings.equityHoldings` object are retained. Yahoo labels the latter `priceToEarnings` and `priceToBook` but reports reciprocal-looking ratios (for example SPY 0.04132 and 0.19034 while quote trailingPE was 23.87835). They are not treated as multiples or inverted to fill missing values. Quote P/E and P/B can differ from holdings statistics. Forward P/E is often missing. No P/E is inferred.

Breadth uses TradingView's undocumented free America screener: listed stocks with market cap at least USD 10bn, including possible foreign listings and multiple share classes. It is a large-cap proxy, not an index constituent list. The close is compared with SMA50, SMA200 and the 52-week high/low, and daily percentage change determines advances and declines. Percentages use the respective observable denominator; unchanged securities count in the change denominator but neither advances nor declines. Extreme counts require an observable close and high/low. The data may be delayed, revised, or unavailable; collection time is not an exchange close timestamp. Europe and Japan are not collected: a comparable verified free universe and complete method have not been established.

Each collection date is Pacific; `fetched_at` is UTC. First successful capture per date and key is immutable, including NULL values. There is no historical backfill. `reader.read_etf_valuations` and `reader.read_market_breadth` accept a UTC `as_of` timestamp, filter by knowledge time, and then select the latest eligible capture. A date-only cutoff means midnight UTC at its start. Coverage appears in the hub coverage report under namespaced keys such as `valuation:SPY` and `breadth:US`, and in the data catalogue. Coverage keys are refreshed on the next coverage rebuild.
